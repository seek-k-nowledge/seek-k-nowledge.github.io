Research note

Why φ-scaling

Chaotic systems — turbulent fluids, weather, robotic control — amplify small errors over time. Standard sequence models lose accuracy quickly as those errors compound. SynechismCore tests whether encoding φ, the golden ratio, as an aperiodic scaling structure inside a Neural ODE helps it hold up longer across these systems. Results are mixed by design: current findings show wins on turbulence and chaotic bifurcation modeling, and a loss on robotics control — reported as-is, not smoothed over.
