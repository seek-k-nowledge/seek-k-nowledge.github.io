Research note

Why φ-scaling

Chaotic systems — turbulent fluids, weather, robotic control — amplify small errors over time, so prediction models drift away from reality. SynechismCore tests whether building φ, the golden ratio, into a Neural ODE as an aperiodic scaling structure helps it stay accurate longer.

Early results were mixed: some wins, and a clear loss on robotics control. Then I found flaws in how the project measured long-horizon accuracy — first in the metric itself, then a hidden 380-step floor in how it reported results. The fix is a floor-free measure, and the experiments are being re-run. Results get reported as they come out — wins and losses both.

How this was made: this site and my projects are built with heavy AI assistance. I direct the work, run it, debug it, and check it — and I'm actively learning to understand every part myself.
